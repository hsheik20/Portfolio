Past · The beginning

Finding my direction.

My journey into programming started through Year Up, where I built a foundation in software development and professional skills. My internship at Microsoft gave me the chance to put that learning into practice, working with data and team reports. Seeing how my work could make information easier to access and analyze helped me realize this was a field I wanted to pursue.

Present · Building on it

Going deeper.

After completing Year Up and my internship, I returned to school to build on that experience. I’m now studying Computer Science at the University of Washington, strengthening my programming skills and exploring analytics, data visualization, and ML/AI. I’m especially drawn to finding patterns in data and presenting them in ways that make sense to others.

Future · What’s next

Making data meaningful.

My goal is to work as a data scientist, combining technical skills with curiosity and clear communication. I want to explore how machine learning and analytics can help solve useful, everyday problems. I’m looking forward to opportunities that challenge me to grow, fit my interests, and let me contribute insights that help people make better decisions.
